About this activity

Saona Luxury is designed for travelers who want more than the standard island tour. This experience combines the natural beauty of Saona Island with a more premium, comfortable, and complete itinerary.

Enjoy stunning Caribbean waters, a natural pool stop, beach time, Mano Juan village, Canto de la Playa, and a more refined island atmosphere. It is ideal for couples, families, and travelers looking for a higher-quality Saona experience.

Itinerary

  • Hotel pickup from Punta Cana area
  • Transfer to the Bayahibe / embarkation area
  • Visit the natural pool with turquoise shallow waters
  • Explore Mano Juan village
  • Visit the turtle sanctuary area
  • Relax at Canto de la Playa
  • Enjoy premium lunch and drinks
  • Return by boat and transfer back to your hotel
