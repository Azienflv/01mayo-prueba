About this activity

Saona Deluxe is ideal for travelers who want the iconic Saona Island adventure with a more professional and comfortable experience than the standard classic tour. You still get the fun Caribbean energy, but with a smoother and more polished feel.

This excursion includes transportation, guide, lunch, drinks, natural pool, catamaran, speed boat, and island time. It is a strong choice for couples, families, and travelers who want great value without choosing the highest luxury level.
