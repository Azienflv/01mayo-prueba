Saona VIP is one of the most requested excursions in Punta Cana for travelers who want something beyond the standard island trip. This experience combines the best of Saona: turquoise waters, natural pools, tropical beaches, catamaran vibes, and Dominican charm, but with a more premium feel from start to finish.
