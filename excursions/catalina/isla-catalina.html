About this activity

Isla Catalina is the perfect day trip for travelers who want clear water, snorkeling, and beach time without the crowds of the bigger island tours. Catalina is a small island off the southeast coast of the Dominican Republic, known for its coral reef, soft white sand, and calm, shallow water.

After your hotel pickup, you will travel to the departure point and sail to the island. Once there, you can snorkel in the reef area, swim, sunbathe, and relax at your own pace before lunch and the return trip. It is a great choice for couples, families, and groups who want a relaxed, scenic Caribbean day.
