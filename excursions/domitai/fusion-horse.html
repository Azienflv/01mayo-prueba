About this activity

Fusion Horseback combines two great outdoor experiences in one tour: the adrenaline of driving buggies or quads through Macao’s private trails and the peaceful beauty of horseback riding through Dominican landscapes.

After the ride, continue to Domitai Park for an organic products tasting experience, a refreshing swim in Cueva Chiquita, and free time at the Dominican Village for shopping, photos, and local souvenirs.

Tour itinerary

  • 7:30 AM - 8:30 AM: Hotel pickups
  • 9:00 AM: Arrival at the ranch, check-in, and safety briefing
  • 9:15 AM: Adventure begins with quad bikes or buggies
  • Horseback riding experience through natural trails
  • 10:00 AM: Arrival at Domitai Park for organic products tasting
  • 10:30 AM: Swim and cool off in the private cenote
  • 11:15 AM: Free time at the Dominican Village for shopping
  • 12:00 PM: Transfer back to hotels

Note: The 2nd and 3rd shifts follow the same program with pickups between 10:30 AM - 11:30 AM and 1:30 PM - 2:30 PM.
