About this activity

Fusion Zipline is designed for travelers who want both adrenaline and scenery. Start with an off-road buggy or quad ride through muddy trails and rural landscapes.

Then take your adventure to the air with a zipline experience across lush tropical terrain. Finish your journey at Domitai Park with a refreshing cenote swim and local tastings.
