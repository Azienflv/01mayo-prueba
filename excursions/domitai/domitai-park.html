About this activity

Welcome to Domitai Park, where adventure meets culture in the heart of the Dominican Republic. This full-day experience combines action, history, nature, food, and entertainment in one powerful tour.

Step into the world of the Taino Indians, taste local organic products, ride through nature, swim in a private cenote cave, enjoy authentic Dominican cuisine, and celebrate with a vibrant carnival show.
