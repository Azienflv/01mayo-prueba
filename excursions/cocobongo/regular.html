About this activity

Coco Bongo Regular is the ideal option for travelers who want to enjoy the iconic Coco Bongo experience with general admission, live performances, music, lights, dancing, and a high-energy party atmosphere.

This ticket includes admission, shows, unlimited national drinks during the included open bar schedule, round-trip transportation, and one snack per person.
