About this activity

El Dorado Water Park is the largest water park in the Caribbean, offering a unique combination of adrenaline, relaxation, and nature.

From extreme slides like “El Huracán” to relaxing rivers and cenotes, this park delivers a full-day experience for all types of travelers.
