About this activity

Enjoy an unforgettable horseback riding experience at Macao Beach, one of the most scenic coastal areas in Punta Cana. Your adventure begins with pickup from your hotel and a comfortable transfer to the ranch area near the beach.

Upon arrival, professional guides will provide a short introduction, safety instructions, and help you feel comfortable with your horse. The ride follows the beach, river area, and natural forest trails at a controlled pace.

What to bring

  • Comfortable closed shoes
  • Camera
  • Sunscreen
  • Water
  • Comfortable clothes
  • Mosquito repellent recommended

Important information

  • Minimum age: 6 years old
  • Children must be accompanied by an adult
  • Maximum permitted weight: 125 kg per person
  • No previous horseback riding experience is required
  • The ride is done at a controlled pace; galloping is not guaranteed
  • Pickup is included from Punta Cana, Bávaro, and Uvero Alto
  • Cap Cana pickup may require an additional fee
  • Exact pickup time is confirmed by WhatsApp or email the day before or the same morning
  • The tour operates in light rain; in bad weather it may be rescheduled or refunded
  • Sunset visibility may vary depending on weather conditions
