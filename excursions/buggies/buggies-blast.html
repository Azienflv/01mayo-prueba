About this activity

Buggies Blast is a high-energy half-day adventure created for travelers who want more than just a ride. Starting from the Macao area, you will drive through muddy private trails, rural landscapes, and exclusive routes designed for maximum fun.

After the off-road action, the experience continues at Domitai Park with tastings of Dominican organic products such as coffee, chocolate, tea, Mamajuana, and more. Then, refresh yourself in the crystal-clear waters of Cueva Chiquita before enjoying free time at the Dominican Village.

Tour itinerary

  • 7:30 AM - 8:30 AM: Hotel pickups
  • 9:00 AM: Arrival at the ranch, check-in, and safety briefing
  • 9:15 AM: Adventure begins with quad bikes or buggies
  • 10:00 AM: Arrival at Domitai Park for organic products tasting
  • 10:30 AM: Swim and cool off in the private cenote
  • 11:15 AM: Free time at the Dominican Village for shopping
  • 12:00 PM: Transfer back to hotels

Note: The 2nd and 3rd shifts follow the same program with pickups between 10:30 AM - 11:30 AM and 1:30 PM - 2:30 PM.
