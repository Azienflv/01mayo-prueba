About this activity

Buggies Classic is one of the most popular adventure tours in Punta Cana. This experience combines off-road driving, countryside views, cultural discovery, Macao Beach, and an optional cave swim in one fun and complete excursion.

Guided by experienced professionals, you will drive through rural trails, muddy roads, and scenic areas before visiting a traditional Dominican stop where you can learn about coffee, cacao, Mamajuana, and hand-rolled cigars.
