About this activity

Buggies Night Unique is an exclusive evening adventure in Punta Cana that combines adrenaline, culture, music, food, and Dominican history under the stars.

Your night begins with a buggy ride through private dirt tracks in Macao. After the off-road adventure, step into Domiati Park to discover a Taino show, enjoy a traditional tasting experience, swim in a cenote cave with DJ atmosphere, and end the night with dinner and a carnival-style show.
