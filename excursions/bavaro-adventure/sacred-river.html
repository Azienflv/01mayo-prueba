About this activity

Some places are not planned — they are revealed. Splash Emotion Sacred River is a nature-focused experience inside Bavaro Adventure Park, created for travelers who want water, relaxation, and tropical scenery.

This tour takes you through underground river passages, semi-enclosed and open cenotes, freshwater lagoons, Jungle River, Waterfall Pool, and a cultural Dominican Village experience.
