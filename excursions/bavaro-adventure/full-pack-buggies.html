Overview Details Good to know

Experience the ultimate all-in-one adventure in Punta Cana. This full-access package combines adrenaline, nature, and culture in one unforgettable day at Bavaro Adventure Park.

Drive through exclusive buggy trails, fly across ziplines, ride horses through natural landscapes, and relax in crystal-clear cenotes — all in one experience designed for travelers who want it all.

🔥 Tour Highlights

  • Off-road buggy adventure through private trails and an abandoned mine
  • 6-line zipline circuit with Mega Splash landing
  • Horseback riding through natural park trails
  • Swim in Cenote Blue Lagoon surrounded by tropical vegetation
  • Relax on the Jungle River floating experience
  • Visit a traditional Dominican village with tastings
  • Waterfall pool to unwind after activities
