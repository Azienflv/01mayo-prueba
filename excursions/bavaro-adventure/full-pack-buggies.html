About this activity

Full Pack Buggies is the ultimate all-in-one adventure package at Bavaro Adventure Park. This experience is designed for travelers who want a complete day of adrenaline, nature, culture, and relaxation without choosing only one activity.

Start with an off-road buggy adventure, continue with a zipline Mega Splash experience, enjoy horseback riding through natural trails, and cool off in cenotes, Jungle River, and the Waterfall Pool. A traditional Dominican lunch is included to complete the day.
