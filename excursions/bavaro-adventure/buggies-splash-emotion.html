About this activity

This experience combines adrenaline and nature in one complete adventure. Start your day driving a buggy through Punta Cana’s off-road trails, mud paths, and scenic tropical landscapes.

Then dive into the Sacred River experience — underground rivers, cenotes, Jungle River, Waterfall Pool, and a Dominican Village visit, all in one unforgettable day at Bavaro Adventure Park.
