About this activity

Triple Adventure is the complete Punta Cana adventure package for travelers who want it all: buggies, ziplines, Monkeyland, Dominican culture, local tastings, lunch, and a swim in the beautiful Anamuya River.

Start your day in the majestic mountains of Anamuya, where specialized guides will explain the safety rules and driving instructions for the Honda Pioneer buggies. Then continue with the zipline circuit, visit Monkeyland, enjoy a Dominican lunch, and finish with Casa del Coco and the Anamuya River.
