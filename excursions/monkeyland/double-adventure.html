About this activity

This Double Adventure is perfect for travelers who want to connect with nature and enjoy two of Punta Cana’s most exciting jungle experiences in one tour. Start with a thrilling zip line circuit high above the rainforest, then continue to Coconut House and Monkeyland.

After pickup from your resort, your instructor will provide helmets, harnesses, and a full safety briefing before the zip line adventure begins. You will fly from platform to platform, enjoying 360-degree jungle views and one zip line stretching more than 2,600 feet.

Monkeyland & Coconut House

After the zip lines, stop at Coconut House to discover how locals transform coconuts into coconut oil and taste some of the Dominican Republic’s best coffee. Then visit Monkeyland, a 5-acre nature park where friendly monkeys may eat from your hands, sit on your shoulders, and create unforgettable photo moments.
