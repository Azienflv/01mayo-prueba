About this activity

Monkeyland is one of the most unique and heartwarming experiences in Punta Cana. This tour takes you into a natural environment where friendly squirrel monkeys roam freely and interact with visitors in a safe and controlled setting.

During your visit, you’ll also discover Dominican culture by visiting a typical countryside house and learning how coffee and cocoa are produced locally. The combination of nature, culture, and wildlife makes this tour perfect for families, couples, and nature lovers.
