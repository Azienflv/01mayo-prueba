About this activity

Enjoy one of the most fun private boat experiences in Punta Cana aboard a two-level yacht designed for music, dancing, swimming, snorkeling, and Caribbean celebration. This is a great choice for birthdays, groups of friends, bachelor parties, family gatherings, or any special occasion.

Your captain and crew will take care of the experience while you enjoy open bar drinks, ocean views, music, snorkeling at the reef, a natural pool stop, and a lively party atmosphere in the shallow turquoise waters of Bávaro.

Experience route

  • First stop: snorkeling adventure at the coral reef
  • Second stop: scenic cruise by Dolphin Island area
  • Third stop: crystal-clear waters for swimming and water slide fun
  • Fourth stop: iconic sandbar and natural pool party
  • Relax and enjoy the floating mat in the Caribbean water
