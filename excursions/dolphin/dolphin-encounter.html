About this activity

Join a family-friendly Dolphin Encounter in Punta Cana and enjoy a memorable 40-minute interaction with one dolphin. This program is perfect for guests of all ages and swimming levels, from beginners to experienced swimmers.

During the experience, you will spend time in the water, receive guidance from professional trainers, and enjoy close interaction moments with a friendly dolphin. It is a great option for families, children, and travelers looking for a gentle and fun dolphin experience.
