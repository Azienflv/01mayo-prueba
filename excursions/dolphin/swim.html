About this activity

Dolphin Swim is a perfect middle option between Dolphin Encounter and Royal Swim. It gives you more interaction than the basic encounter while keeping the experience accessible, fun, and family-friendly.

During this program, expert trainers will guide you through the activity and help you interact safely with one dolphin. You will spend 40 minutes in the water learning, swimming, and enjoying unforgettable moments with one of the ocean’s most intelligent animals.
