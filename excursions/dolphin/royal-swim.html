About this activity

Dolphin Royal Swim is one of the most complete dolphin experiences in Punta Cana. This program is designed for guests who want more action, closer interaction, and unforgettable moments with these intelligent marine animals.

During the experience, expert trainers will guide you through the activity and show you how to interact safely with the dolphins. You will enjoy fun moments in the water, learn about dolphin behavior, and experience exciting activities such as the foot push and dorsal ride.
