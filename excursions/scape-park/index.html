Scape Park

Explore caves, cenotes, zip lines, and unforgettable adventure attractions in one of Punta Cana’s top parks.

Experience Overview

Scape Park offers a complete day of adventure with zip lines, caves, trails, and the famous Hoyo Azul. It is one of the most exciting nature and adrenaline experiences in Punta Cana.
