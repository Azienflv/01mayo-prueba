About this activity

Scape Park Punta Cana is a full-day eco-adventure park designed for travelers who want nature, adrenaline, culture, and relaxation in one place. From zip lining through the jungle to swimming in crystal-clear freshwater cenotes, this park offers a complete day of unforgettable experiences.

Explore underground caves, discover the famous Blue Hole, enjoy jungle trails, waterfalls, animal areas, and adventure attractions surrounded by the natural beauty of Cap Cana. It is a great choice for families, couples, and adventure lovers.

Important information

  • Wear clothes that can get dirty and bring swimsuit, towel, flip flops or water shoes
  • Bring sunglasses, sunscreen, insect repellent, and additional cash
  • Bring cash or credit card for food, drinks, souvenirs, and personal expenses
  • Pregnant women are not permitted on this tour
  • Guests with back issues should seek medical advice before participating
  • Guests under the influence of alcohol or drugs will not be allowed to participate
  • Some attractions have minimum age, height, and weight requirements

Age and activity requirements

  • Cave Expedition: minimum age 6 years old
  • Zip Line Eco Splash: minimum age 6 years old
  • Zip Line Eco Splash typical range: 1.2–1.98 m height and 27–127 kg weight
  • Cave Swim: minimum age 5 years old
  • Blue Hole Sinkhole: minimum age 2 years old

Transportation notes

  • Transportation is provided by the activity partner in their vehicle
  • An additional $10 USD fee may apply for selected Bayahibe / Dominicus area hotels
  • A $15 USD transportation supplement may apply from La Romana resorts
  • Pickup time depends on your hotel location
