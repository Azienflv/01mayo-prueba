About this activity

Safari Punta Cana is an authentic countryside experience designed for travelers who want to see the real Dominican Republic beyond the beach resorts. This full-day adventure combines culture, agriculture, nature, local flavors, and traditional Dominican lifestyle.

You will visit rural areas, learn about sugar cane production, see how local tobacco is transformed into handmade cigars, enjoy a horseback ride through scenic fields, and relax with a traditional Dominican buffet lunch surrounded by tropical nature.

Experience details

  • Walk through a sugar cane plantation and learn about traditional farming methods
  • See how sugar cane becomes juice, molasses, and Dominican rum
  • Visit local artisans rolling premium cigars with locally harvested tobacco
  • Enjoy freshly brewed coffee, pure cacao, and tropical fruits
  • Explore the countryside with a relaxing horseback ride
  • Visit a typical Dominican home and learn about local traditions
