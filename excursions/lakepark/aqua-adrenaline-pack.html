About this activity

Aqua Adrenaline is designed for travelers who want nonstop action, speed, and water-based fun. This package brings together some of the most exciting attractions at Caribbean Lake Park, including Jet Ski, Aqua Karts, Buggies, Donut Ride, Zipline, and inflatable obstacles.

Whether you are traveling with friends, family, or as a solo adventurer, this experience is built to create unforgettable memories with a mix of water thrills, off-road excitement, and tropical park vibes.
