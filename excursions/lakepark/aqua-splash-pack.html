About this activity

Aqua Splash Pack is a refreshing water adventure designed for guests who want a fun, active, and easygoing experience at Caribbean Lake Park. The main attraction is a 20-minute Jet Ski Fun ride, combined with extra activities like Donut Ride, Zipline, and the inflatable obstacle circuit.

After the action, enjoy access to the Pool Club and the park’s relaxation areas. It is a perfect option for families, couples, and groups who want water fun without the full extreme package.
