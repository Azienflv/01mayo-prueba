About this activity

Aqua Adventure Pack is designed for those who want a mix of action and fun without going full extreme. You will enjoy Buggies and Jet Ski as the main activities, combined with additional attractions that keep the excitement going.

This experience is perfect for families, couples, and groups looking for a dynamic but balanced adventure day in Punta Cana.
