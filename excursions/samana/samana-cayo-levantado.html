About this activity

Discover the beauty of Cayo Levantado, also known as Bacardí Island, with a relaxing full-day excursion. This experience combines scenic views, beach time, and Caribbean vibes in one unforgettable day.

Departing from Samaná at 9:30 AM, you will enjoy a smooth boat ride to the island. Once there, you are free to swim, sunbathe, explore, or simply relax while enjoying the tropical atmosphere.
