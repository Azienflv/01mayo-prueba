About this activity

This Samaná full-day experience combines beach, nature, culture, and adventure in one complete excursion. Start with comfortable transportation from the Punta Cana or Bayahibe area and continue to Miches, where you will board a boat toward Cayo Levantado.

Enjoy approximately 3 hours on Cayo Levantado with time for lunch, beach relaxation, coconut trees, and a refreshing swim. Later, continue toward Playa Rincón and Caño Frío, where you can explore white-sand beaches and ride a traditional canoe through the mangroves.

Experience route

  • Departure from Punta Cana or Bayahibe area
  • Comfortable transfer in modern air-conditioned buses
